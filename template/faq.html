Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the questions our candidates ask most often

How can I trust you?

Our reputation is built on years of experience, absolute legal compliance, and high standards of service for our candidates and partners. We operate through officially registered corporate entities and physical offices in Europe (including Poland and Ukraine), ensuring all financial settlements are conducted securely via official bank accounts. To provide complete peace of mind, we welcome direct communication—whether through a personal Zoom consultation, sharing our official registration details, or welcoming your relatives or friends to visit our European offices in person. Transparency and reliability are the cornerstones of our business.

How do I choose the right country and what are my visa success chances?

Visa success depends on individual background, document history, and embassy requirements. The most critical factor is not simply choosing a country with high approval rates, but identifying where you can successfully secure an embassy appointment and complete the process. Our experienced managers evaluate your profile individually and recommend the most realistic and optimal destinations.

Who submits the visa application and do you book embassy appointments?

According to European Union immigration regulations, candidates must personally apply for their work visa through the designated embassy or visa application center in accordance with territorial jurisdiction rules. Our responsibility is to prepare and facilitate all employer-side documents and work permits. Because each country has distinct appointment systems and booking rules, we do not handle embassy appointment bookings as part of our standard service, though we guide you thoroughly through the process.

Why can't document preparation be faster or job start dates changed easily?

Document preparation involves official approvals from government authorities and the employer, which follow strict legal timelines that we cannot bypass. Similarly, changing a job start date is not simple because the work permit and employment contract are tied to official government filings; altering the date requires updating and reissuing multiple official documents.

Are your services paid, and how does the payment process work?

Yes, our services are paid. Legal employment abroad involves substantial upfront expenses during the preparation phase, including official government fees, registration charges, work permit processing, employer-side administrative costs, and comprehensive support from our managers. Therefore, document processing begins upon an initial payment, with subsequent payments structured around key documentation milestones.

What documents are required from a candidate to start the process?

To initiate the process, only a valid international passport is strictly required. Depending on the destination country, specific vacancy, and employer requirements, additional information such as proof of address, registration details, a CV, or qualification certificates may be requested. Following an initial consultation, our manager will provide you with an exact, personalized checklist tailored to your case.

How can you guarantee that a candidate will not be left without a job?

We carefully verify every employer and vacancy before presentation, confirming job conditions, salaries, and employment details in advance. We maintain ongoing communication with employers throughout the entire recruitment cycle and support candidates if any unexpected issues arise, ensuring a safe, transparent, and reliable process.

Can I communicate directly with the employer or their representative?

Communication with the employer is coordinated exclusively through our agency during the preparation phase to ensure accuracy and consistency. Once candidates arrive and commence employment, they have full access to communicate directly with the employer and their representatives regarding daily work and responsibilities.
