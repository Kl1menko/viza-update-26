How to get a job in Ukraine

  1. Consultation: our manager reviews your profile and recommends the most suitable vacancy in Ukraine.
  2. Passport: to start, only a valid international passport is required; other documents depend on the vacancy.
  3. Work permit: we prepare the employer documents and the work permit for you.
  4. Work visa: you apply for the work visa in person at the embassy or visa center, and we guide you through every step.
  5. Arrival: you start working, and our team stays in touch to support you if any issues arise.
