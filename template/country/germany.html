Jobs in Germany for foreigners: work visa, salary and housing

Viza Work Agency helps foreign workers find legal jobs in Germany. Current vacancies include cleaning worker and warehouse worker, with salaries up to 2,600 EUR per month, and accommodation is provided.

Document validity: Long-term employment contracts. Key advantages: highest European salaries, high living standards.

How to get a job in Germany

  1. Consultation: our manager reviews your profile and recommends the most suitable vacancy in Germany.
  2. Passport: to start, only a valid international passport is required; other documents depend on the vacancy.
  3. Work permit: we prepare the employer documents and the work permit for you.
  4. Work visa: you apply for the work visa in person at the embassy or visa center, and we guide you through every step.
  5. Arrival: you start working, and our team stays in touch to support you if any issues arise.
