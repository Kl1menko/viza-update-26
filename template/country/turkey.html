Jobs in Turkey for foreigners: work visa, salary and housing

Viza Work Agency helps foreign workers find legal jobs in Turkey. Current vacancies include hotel & restaurant staff and factory & agricultural worker, with salaries up to 880 EUR per month, and accommodation is provided.

Document validity: 1-year work visa / permit. Key advantages: free housing & medical insurance, fast entry.

How to get a job in Turkey

  1. Consultation: our manager reviews your profile and recommends the most suitable vacancy in Turkey.
  2. Passport: to start, only a valid international passport is required; other documents depend on the vacancy.
  3. Work permit: we prepare the employer documents and the work permit for you.
  4. Work visa: you apply for the work visa in person at the embassy or visa center, and we guide you through every step.
  5. Arrival: you start working, and our team stays in touch to support you if any issues arise.
