Frequently Asked Questions

FAQ for employers

Which Asian and African countries do you work with?

We directly cooperate with countries in South and Southeast Asia (specifically India and Bangladesh) and cover the entire African region, selecting the best and most motivated candidates.

How do candidate interviews take place?

We organize direct online meetings (video interviews) where you can personally speak with a potential employee. Before that, we thoroughly check their resume, work experience, and language proficiency.

Do you handle visa processing and legalization?

Yes, we fully accompany the entire complex of migration and legal processes. Our team manages work visa processing, preparation of the required document package, interaction with embassies, and compliance with all local legal requirements.

How does candidate adaptation happen?

We prepare specialists for integration into the European working environment, explain all organizational nuances, and support them during the relocation phase, ensuring smooth and fast adaptation in the new place.
