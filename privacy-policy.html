1. Who we are

This website is operated by Viza Work Agency (“we”, “us”), an agency that has helped people find legal employment in Europe since 2016. We have offices in Warsaw (Poland), Lviv (Ukraine) and Valencia (Spain).

The controller of your personal data is the Viza Work Agency company that you communicate with or sign an agreement with. Its full legal details are stated in your agreement, and we will provide them on request at any time.

For any question about your personal data or to exercise your rights, send us a message through the contact form on this website, contact your manager by phone or messenger, or write to us on our social media pages. Please mention “Personal data” in your message so that we can handle it as a priority.

2. Scope of this policy

This policy explains how we process personal data in line with Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (General Data Protection Regulation, “GDPR”) and applicable national law. It applies to:

  • job candidates who contact us or use our employment and work visa support services;
  • employers and their representatives who ask us to recruit workers;
  • partner agencies and their representatives;
  • visitors of www.vizaworkagency.com.

3. What data we collect

Data you give us through the website forms

  • Job inquiry form: full name, phone number (WhatsApp), email address, country of origin and your message.
  • Partner form: contact person, company name, company website or social media, phone number (WhatsApp), email address, countries you have worked with or are interested in, and your message.
  • Employer form: name of the contact person, company name and role, country where the company is located, business sector, phone (Telegram or WhatsApp) or email, number of workers and details of the request.

Each form also sends the address of the page it was submitted from, so we know which vacancy or service you are asking about.

Data we collect while providing our services to candidates

If you decide to work with us, we ask for the data needed to find you a job and prepare your documents, for example: date of birth, nationality, passport details and a copy of your passport, CV, education and work experience, language skills, photographs, current address, marital status where required by the visa application, and copies of diplomas, certificates and permits. We collect this data from you directly. In some cases we receive information from the employer you are interviewing with (for example, interview results) or from public authorities (for example, the status of a permit application).

Communication data

When you contact us by phone, email, WhatsApp, Telegram, Viber or social media, we process your contact details and the content of the conversation.

Technical data

When you visit the website, your browser automatically sends technical data such as your IP address, browser type, device and the pages you visit. See Cookies and analytics.

5. Sensitive data and criminal records

We do not ask for data about health, religion, ethnic origin or other special categories through the website. Please do not include such data in your messages.

Some countries require a medical certificate or a criminal record certificate for a work permit or visa. In such cases we process these documents only to the extent required by the law of the destination country, only to submit them to the competent authority or employer, and only with your explicit consent (Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR) or where the processing is authorised by law (Art. 10 GDPR).

6. Who we share data with

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only when necessary for the purposes above, with:

  • employers who are considering your candidacy, and only with your knowledge;
  • public authorities such as voivodeship offices, labour offices, consulates and immigration services, when we submit applications for you;
  • partner agencies that help us with a specific recruitment or document process;
  • service providers who process data on our behalf under a data processing agreement: website hosting, email and form handling, cloud storage, CRM, messaging services, accounting and legal advisers;
  • Microsoft (Clarity analytics) and jsDelivr (the content delivery network that serves the phone number field on our forms), which receive technical data such as your IP address when you use the website.

7. Transfers outside the EEA

Because of the nature of our services, some data may be transferred outside the European Economic Area: to our office in Ukraine, to employers or partners located outside the EEA, and to service providers based in the United States.

We make such transfers only when they are protected by an adequacy decision of the European Commission (for example, the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework for certified companies) or by the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses (Art. 46 GDPR). Where neither is possible, a transfer may take place because it is necessary to perform a contract concluded with you or in your interest, for example to submit your documents to an employer or consulate you chose (Art. 49(1)(b) and (c) GDPR). You can ask us for a copy of the safeguards we use.

8. How long we keep data

  • Inquiries that did not lead to cooperation: up to 12 months from our last contact.
  • Candidate data processed under a contract: for the duration of the contract and then until the expiry of limitation periods for claims, usually 3 to 6 years.
  • Candidate profile kept for future vacancies: up to 2 years, or until you withdraw your consent.
  • Accounting documents: 5 years from the end of the year in which the tax payment deadline expired, as required by Polish law.
  • Employer and partner contact data: for the duration of the cooperation and then until the expiry of limitation periods for claims.
  • Analytics data: as described in Cookies and analytics.

After these periods, we delete or anonymise the data.

9. Your rights

Under the GDPR, you have the right to:

  • access your data and receive a copy of it;
  • rectify inaccurate or incomplete data;
  • erase your data (“right to be forgotten”);
  • restrict processing;
  • data portability, that is, receive the data you gave us in a machine-readable format or have it sent to another controller;
  • object to processing based on our legitimate interest;
  • withdraw consent at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal.

To exercise these rights, contact us through any of the channels listed in section 1. We will reply within one month. In complex cases this period may be extended by two more months, and we will let you know. We may ask you to confirm your identity before acting on a request.

10. Complaints to a supervisory authority

If you believe we process your data unlawfully, you have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority. Our lead supervisory authority is the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (Prezes Urzędu Ochrony Danych Osobowych), ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warsaw, Poland, uodo.gov.pl.

You may also complain to the authority in the EU country where you live or work, for example the Spanish Data Protection Agency (aepd.es). If you are in Ukraine, you may also contact the Ukrainian Parliament Commissioner for Human Rights (ombudsman.gov.ua).

We would appreciate the chance to resolve your concern first, so please contact us before going to an authority.

11. Cookies and analytics

Cookies are small files that a website stores on your device. Our website does not set its own cookies. It uses one third-party service that does:

  • Microsoft Clarity, an analytics service provided by Microsoft Corporation, which helps us understand how visitors use the website through usage statistics, heatmaps and session recordings. Clarity uses cookies and similar technologies and collects data such as clicks, scrolling, the pages you visit, the type of device and an approximate location based on your IP address. Text you type into form fields is masked and not recorded. Session recordings are kept for 30 days and aggregated statistics for up to 13 months. More information: Microsoft Privacy Statement.

You can delete cookies and block them at any time in your browser settings. Blocking analytics cookies does not affect how the website works.

12. Security

We use technical and organisational measures appropriate to the risk to protect your data: an encrypted connection (HTTPS), access limited to staff who need the data for their work and who are bound by confidentiality, data processing agreements with our service providers, and regular review of the data we keep.

13. Other information

Our services are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect data of persons under 18.

Our website contains links to social networks such as Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn and TikTok. When you visit our profiles there, the operator of the network also processes your data under its own privacy policy.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our services or the law change. The current version is always available on this page, with the date of the last update at the top.
